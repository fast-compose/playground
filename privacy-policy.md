<h1 align=center>Fast Compose Playground - Privacy Policy</h1>

<div class="box">

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

## Privacy Policy

Fast Compose Playground does not ask for an account and does not collect or transfer any personal information.

## Data kept on your device

The app remembers your last playground on your device, so it comes back the next time you open the app:

- the code in the editor
- the theme, the font size and the layout of the panes

On the web, the same data is kept in your browser's local storage and in the page address. Nothing of it is sent to
us. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's data or your browser's site data.

## Shared links

On the web, the Share button copies a link that holds your code. Only the people you send the link to can see it.

## Network access

The app uses the internet only to:

- load the images your code asks for, such as `AsyncImage(model = "https://...")`, from the address you wrote
- open the links you tap, such as GitHub, in your browser

## Third party libraries

The Google Play version of the app uses third-party services that may collect information about the app's state and
the device, such as crash reports and anonymous usage statistics:

<ul>
  <li>
    <a href="https://firebase.google.com/docs/android/play-data-disclosure#crashlytics">Firebase Crashlytics</a>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11582702">Firebase Analytics</a>
  </li>
</ul>

The web, desktop and other Android versions of the app do not use these services.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
